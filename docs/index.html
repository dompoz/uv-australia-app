Privacy Policy for UV Australia

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Overview

UV Australia is an app that provides real-time UV index information for your location in Australia. This privacy policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Location

The app requests access to your device's precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) solely to determine which UV monitoring station to retrieve data from. Your location is used on-device to make a request to the UV data service and is not stored, logged, or transmitted to us. You may revoke location permission at any time in your device settings, though this will prevent the app from fetching UV readings.

UV Data

The app fetches UV index data from the Australian Radiation Protection and Nuclear Safety Agency (ARPANSA) public data service. No personally identifiable information is sent as part of these requests.

App Settings

Settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore and are never transmitted anywhere.

Crash Reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports when the app unexpectedly closes. These reports help us identify and fix bugs. Crash reports may include:

  • Device model and Android version
  • App version
  • The state of the app at the time of the crash (stack traces and logs)
  • A randomly generated installation ID (not linked to your identity)

Crash data is processed by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymised usage data, including:

  • App open and session events
  • Device type and Android version
  • General country/region (not precise location)

This data is aggregated and anonymised. It is used only to understand how the app is used in general and to guide improvements. It is processed by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or files
  • We do not sell any data to third parties
  • Your precise location is used only on-device and is never sent to our servers

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • ARPANSA UV data service — public UV index data for Australian monitoring stations
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash reporting
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — anonymised usage analytics

Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics are operated by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

UV Australia does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The data collected by Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics is anonymised and not linked to any individual user.

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Play Store release notes.
